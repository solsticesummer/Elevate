The science

Built on chronobiology.
Not guesswork.

Every recommendation Circadia makes is grounded in peer-reviewed circadian biology research.

☀️

Light exposure is the master reset

The suprachiasmatic nucleus responds to light more than any other signal. Circadia times your light and dark exposure to shift your clock faster.

🍽️

Peripheral clocks follow food timing

Organs throughout your body keep their own time, and meal timing is their primary cue. Eating at the right hours accelerates your circadian adjustment.

💊

Melatonin timing beats dosage

Research shows that when you take melatonin matters far more than how much. A precisely timed low dose outperforms a large random one.

🦉

Chronotype changes the entire strategy

Night owls and early birds have different phase-shifting curves. The same flight requires a completely different schedule for each chronotype.

24-hour circadian cycle

Your body clock runs on a precise ~24.2 hour cycle, governed by light, food, and temperature cues. Circadia uses all three.
